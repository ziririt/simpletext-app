Paste messy AI text. Get a clean note.

Skyblue Note is a simple notes app with one job:
take answers from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, or Perplexity
and remove the markdown symbols, extra bold marks, and broken tables.

WHAT IT DOES
• Paste an AI reply and strip the markdown junk
• Repair broken tables and copy them straight into a spreadsheet
• Write on paper-like notes with tags and search
• Sync across iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android and the web with Google Drive — iCloud works too on Apple devices
• Lock your notes with Face ID

WHAT IT IS NOT
Not Notion. Not another AI chatbot.
Just a quiet place to keep text after the AI is done talking.

HOW TO USE
1. Copy an answer from your AI app
2. Paste it into Skyblue Note
3. Read a clean note. Keep writing.

Made in Korea. Available worldwide.
